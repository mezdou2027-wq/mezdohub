## 2. Sectoral Transition Trajectories

### Trajectory A: The OFPPT Vocational Path (ISTA)
* **Year 0:** Technicien Spécialisé Diploma in Électromécanique / Câblage.
* **Years 1–3:** Entry at Lear / Stellantis / Renault as Opérateur or Technicien Maintenance Junior (3,800 to 5,500 MAD).
* **Years 4–5 (Fork in the Road):**
  * *Option 1 (Domestic Seniority):* Transition to Chef d'Équipe / Superviseur in Midparc Casablanca or Tanger Med (7,500 to 11,000 MAD).
  * *Option 2 (Public Sector):* Pass OFPPT Formateur competition (Échelle 9/10).
  * *Option 3 (International):* Apply via Germany Chancenkarte (qualifies for 6 points with 5-year experience).

### Trajectory B: The University Graduate Path (Licence / Master)
* **Option 1 (Civil Service):** Concours AREF Education (Échelle 10) or Municipalities Administrateur 3ème Grade.
* **Option 2 (Private Sector):** Customer Success & BPO in Casablanca / Tangier (6,000 to 8,000 MAD) or Junior Banking in CFC.
