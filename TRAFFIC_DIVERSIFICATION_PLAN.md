## 3. Pillar 1: DV Lottery Traffic Strategy (The High-Velocity Hook)

* **Strategic Role:** **Top-of-funnel explosive user acquisition & fast cash flow.**
* **Target Audience:** Moroccan youth, university students, and mobility seekers.
* **Traffic Acquisition Vectors:**
  1. *Seasonal Viral Video Shorts:* TikTok Darija demos showing real photo failures turning green.
  2. *Facebook Community Seeding:* High-value advisory warnings in 180,000+ member DV lottery groups.
  3. *WhatsApp Peer Cascades:* One-click WhatsApp share button on the scan result screen.
* **The Bridge Mechanism (Traffic Recycling):**
  * Every applicant who tests their photo is immediately presented with:
    > *"بينما تنتظر نتائج القرعة الأمريكية، جهز سيرتك الذاتية المهنية الدولية بالمعايير الكندية والمغربية للتقديم على كبريات الشركات: [صانع السيرة الذاتية ATS]"*
  * **Target Conversion:** Convert **20% of DV visitors into registered candidates** who build an ATS resume on MezdoHub.

## 4. Pillar 2: ATS Resume Builder Traffic Strategy (The Evergreen Utility)

* **Strategic Role:** **Stable, predictable, month-over-month organic search volume.**
* **Target Audience:** OFPPT / ISTA vocational students, fresh graduates, active job seekers.
* **Search Volume Baseline in Morocco:** Over **120,000 monthly searches** year-round across queries like `créer un cv marocain gratuit`, `modèle cv ofppt gratuit`, `cv format canadien maroc`, and `نموذج سيرة ذاتية مغربية`.
* **Traffic Acquisition Vectors:**
  1. *Programmatic SEO Landing Pages:* Dedicated indexable pages for `modèle cv technicien électromécanique`, `cv câblage tanger`, and `cv logistique portuaire`.
  2. *Higher Education & Vocational Partnerships:* Seeding free PDF templates in ISTA alumni WhatsApp groups and Telegram channels at the start of each academic semester (September, January, and June PFE season).
  3. *LinkedIn Recruiter Advocacy:* Articles showing why recruiters reject Canva CVs and prefer 1-column ATS PDFs.
* **Retention Loop:** Resumes are saved locally or in user profiles, prompting users to return every time they apply to a new job.

## 5. Pillar 3: Local Jobs Hub Traffic Strategy (The High-Revenue Marketplace)

* **Strategic Role:** **Direct B2B monetization, employer lead generation, and recurring daily active users (DAU).**
* **Target Audience:** Technicians, operators, engineers, and plant managers in Tangier, Kenitra, and Casablanca.
* **Traffic Acquisition Vectors:**
  1. *Google for Jobs Integration (`JobPosting` Schema):* Captures high-intent intent queries like `emploi usine tanger` and `recrutement renault melloussa` directly in Google search cards.
  2. *Weekly "Tangier Industrial Hiring Roundup":* Published every Monday morning across WhatsApp groups and Facebook job boards, summarizing open plant vacancies at Lear, Aptiv, Yazaki, and Renault.
  3. *Employer Referral Networks:* B2B recruiters sharing MezdoHub job cards directly on LinkedIn because the platform pre-screens candidates with ATS-ready files.
* **Monetization Engine:**
  * Candidates pay for **Direct HR Routing (49 MAD)**.
  * Companies pay for **Sponsored Job Slots (490 MAD)**.

## 6. Strategic Traffic Allocation Model

To guarantee long-term operational health, MezdoHub will allocate marketing and promotional resources across three distinct traffic classifications:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   PORTFOLIO TRAFFIC ALLOCATION MATRIX                  │
├──────────────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────┬─────────────────────┤
│ Traffic Category     │ Current Mix │ Target (M3) │ Primary Driver      │
├──────────────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────────────┤
│ 1. Short-Term Viral  │ 65.0%       │ 20.0%       │ DV Lottery Scanner  │
│ 2. Evergreen Search  │ 20.0%       │ 50.0%       │ ATS CV & Guides     │
│ 3. Commercial/Revenue│ 15.0%       │ 30.0%       │ Tangier Jobs & B2B  │
├──────────────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────┼─────────────────────┤
│ TOTAL                │ 100.0%      │ 100.0%      │ BALANCED PLATFORM   │
└──────────────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────┴─────────────────────┘
```

### Definitions:
* **Short-Term Traffic (Viral):** High-velocity bursts. Cost of acquisition is $0, but lifespan is short. Used solely to build brand awareness, backlinks, and first-time users.
* **Evergreen Traffic (Organic Search):** Passive, compounding Google traffic. Users seeking tools, calculators, and resume templates every day of the year.
* **Revenue Traffic (Commercial Intent):** High-converting visitors with immediate commercial intent (applying for jobs, purchasing VIP application packages, or hiring staff).
