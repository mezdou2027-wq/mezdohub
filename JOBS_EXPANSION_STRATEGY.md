**Document Version:** 1.0.0  
**Target Regions:** Tangier-Tetouan-Al Hoceima, Rabat-Salé-Kénitra, Casablanca-Settat

## 2. Sectoral Taxonomies by Metropolitan Zone

### A) Tangier Metropolitan Zone
* **Automotive & Wiring:** Renault Melloussa, Lear Corporation (TAC/TFZ), Yazaki, Aptiv, Antolin, Valeo.
* **Logistics & Maritime:** Tanger Med Port Authority, APM Terminals, DHL Global Forwarding, Geodis.
* **Textile & Technical Apparel:** Dalsan, Inditex sourcing partners, Textilver.
* **Customer Care / BPO:** Teleperformance, Majorel/Teleperformance, Webhelp.

### B) Kenitra Metropolitan Zone (Atlantic Free Zone - AFZ)
* **Automotive Assembly:** Stellantis Kenitra Plant (Peugeot 208, Citroën Ami, Fiat Topolino).
* **Automotive Tier-1 Suppliers:** Nexteer Automotive (Steering systems), AGC Automotive (Safety glass), Kromberg & Schubert (Wiring systems), Fujikura, Hirschmann Automotive.
* **Agri-Food Processing:** Kenitra Agropolis industrial cluster.

### C) Casablanca Metropolitan Zone
* **Aerospace & Defence (Midparc Nouaceur):** Safran Nacelles, Boeing Morocco, Hexcel, Collins Aerospace, Daher.
* **Technology & Offshoring (Casanearshore):** Capgemini, Atos, DXC Technology, Inetum, IBM Morocco.
* **Banking & Fintech (Casablanca Finance City):** Attijariwafa bank, BCP, Bank of Africa, CIH Bank, CDG Capital.
* **Retail, FMCG & Telecom:** Marjane Group, Label'Vie (Carrefour), Centrale Danone, Orange Maroc, inwi.

## 3. Data Integrity & Verification Standards

To uphold journalistic and technical authenticity:
* **[VERIFIED]:** Verified company legal names, exact plant locations, formal diplomas demanded, and confirmed public portal addresses.
* **[ESTIMATED]:** Salary ranges based on national collective bargaining conventions (*Conventions Collectives*), SMIG benchmarks, and industry recruiter surveys.
* **[PLACEHOLDER]:** Internal tracking identifiers and temporary batch IDs.
