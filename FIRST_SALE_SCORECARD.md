## 4. Key Behavioral Lessons from Day 1 Leads

1. **Lesson 1: The 25 MAD Price Point Has Zero Resistance**
   * Lead #1 (`dv-lead-01` from Tangier) did not negotiate or ask for a discount. Upon reading that AI tools risk EXIF disqualification, their immediate response was: *"D'accord, c'est pas cher. Donnez-moi le RIB CIH"*.
2. **Lesson 2: Evening Drop-off & Morning Settlement Friction**
   * Late evening inquiries (post 21:30) often stall at the payment step because users prefer to do bank transfers during the day or when on Wi-Fi/desktop. Follow-ups must be timed for 10:00 AM.
3. **Lesson 3: The Second Lead Objection (`dv-lead-02`)**
   * The second applicant wanted to know if 25 MAD included entering their personal data on the official government website. Clarifying that official entry opens in October and photo preparation is Step 1 will turn them into a full VIP package lead.
