By deploying hyper-targeted free utilities directly into active niche groups, the first 100 users are acquired organically in **under 7 days**.

3. **Freelance & Digital Economy Groups:**
   * *Freelancers Morocco / المقاول الذاتي بالمغرب* (85,000+ members)

### Rules of Engagement (Anti-Spam Discipline):
* **Never drop raw links alone.** Always write a value-first 4-paragraph post explaining a real technical tip (e.g., why photoshop breaks EXIF data).
* **Engage in the comments first:** Answer 3 questions from other users before introducing the MezdoHub free tool.
* Post during Moroccan peak hours: **12:30 PM – 2:00 PM** (lunch break) and **8:30 PM – 11:00 PM** (evening relaxation).
