## 4. B2B Employer & Recruiter Revenue Streams

Industrial plants in Tangier Automotive City (TAC), Tangier Free Zone (TFZ), and Casablanca Shore face massive turnover and spend thousands of Dirhams sourcing reliable technicians.

1. **Featured Job Slot (Offre d'Emploi Sponsorisée):**
   * Pinned at the top of the MezdoHub homepage and job marketplace for 30 days.
   * Highlighted with "Entreprise Vérifiée" badge.
   * Broadcasted to MezdoHub WhatsApp & Telegram channels (5,000+ candidates).
   * *Price:* **490 MAD per job listing** (vs. 1,500+ MAD on Rekrute/Bayt).
2. **Access to Pre-Screened Candidate Database (CVthèque Industrielle):**
   * Recruiter dashboard allowing instant search by diploma (`technicien_specialise_ista`), specialization (`Electromécanique`), city, and language skills.
   * *Subscription:* **1,490 MAD / month** (includes unlimited candidate contacts).
3. **Turnkey Sourcing Blast (Campagne de Recrutement Express):**
   * Dedicated email & WhatsApp campaign to 1,000 targeted technicians matching the plant's exact criteria.
   * Guaranteed delivery of 30 qualified candidate dossiers within 72 hours.
   * *Price:* **2,900 MAD per campaign**.

## 5. Strategic Affiliate & Partner Opportunities

MezdoHub can generate high-margin passive affiliate revenue by recommending trusted tools:

1. **Fintech & Cross-Border Payment Cards (Wise & Payoneer):**
   * Driven from the PayPal to CIH calculator.
   * Recommendation: *"Avoid PayPal's 3.5% conversion spread by using Wise / Payoneer to withdraw directly to Morocco."*
   * *Bounty:* **$25 to $50 (250–500 MAD)** per funded card account.
2. **Call Center / BPO Hiring Bounties:**
   * Major call centers in Casablanca and Tangier (Teleperformance, Majorel, Webhelp/Concentrix) offer referral bonuses for hiring French/English agents.
   * MezdoHub captures applicants through its language tests and refers them directly to BPO recruitment teams.
   * *Bounty:* **500 to 1,000 MAD** per successfully hired candidate.
3. **Certified Legal Sworn Translation Partners:**
   * Candidates applying to Canada or foreign visas need official sworn translations of their OFPPT diplomas and police records.
   * Partner with certified translators in Tangier/Casablanca.
   * *Commission:* **20% per translation order**.
