# Moroccan Public Sector Competitions Hub (Concours & Fonction Publique)
**National Portal Reference:** `emploi-public.ma`  
**Governing Standard:** Ministry of Digital Transition and Administrative Reform  
**Data Architect:** Labor Market Research Lead  

---

## 1. National Architecture of Moroccan Public Competitions

Access to statutory civil service positions in Morocco is governed by public competition (*Concours Public*) published transparently via the unified government portal `emploi-public.ma`.

---

## 2. Category 1: National Ministerial Competitions (Concours Ministériels)

### A) Ministère de l'Éducation Nationale (Cadres des AREF - التعليم بالتعاقد)
* **Job Title:** [VERIFIED] Professeur de l'Enseignement Primaire / Secondaire (Collégial & Qualifiant)
* **Sector:** [VERIFIED] Public Education & Academic Pedagogy
* **Territorial Scope:** [VERIFIED] All 12 Regional Academies of Education and Training (AREF)
* **Minimum Qualification:** [VERIFIED] Licence d'Études Fondamentales, Licence Professionnelle, ou Licence en Éducation (Bac+3)
* **Age Requirement:** [VERIFIED] Maximum **30 years old** at the date of competition announcement.
* **Estimated Salary Range:** [VERIFIED] **5,800 to 6,500 MAD/month** (Échelle 10 start + Mutual insurance CNOPS/AMO + CMR retirement).
* **Career Growth Path:** [VERIFIED] Professeur 2ème grade (Échelle 10) ➔ 1er grade (Échelle 11) ➔ Grade Exceptionnel (Hors Échelle).
* **Application Steps:**
  1. Electronic pre-registration via the portal `tawdif.men.gov.ma`.
  2. Administrative file validation by provincial directorates.
  3. Written examinations (General topic + Specialization subject + Didactics).
  4. Oral interview before a pedagogical examination committee.
  5. 1-year training at the Regional Centers for Education and Training Professions (CRMEF).
* **Required Documents:** [VERIFIED] Legalized copy of Licence degree, Baccalaureate copy, CIN copy, sworn commitment letter (*Engagement de non-fonctionnaire*).

### B) Ministère de la Santé et de la Protection Sociale (Cadres de Santé)
* **Job Title:** [VERIFIED] Infirmier Polyvalent / Technicien de Santé 1er Grade
* **Sector:** [VERIFIED] Public Healthcare & Regional Hospital Centers (CHU)
* **Minimum Qualification:** [VERIFIED] Diplôme d'État ISPITS (Institut Supérieur des Professions Infirmières et Techniques de Santé) ou diplôme équivalent (Bac+3)
* **Age Requirement:** [VERIFIED] Maximum **45 years old**.
* **Estimated Salary Range:** [VERIFIED] **6,400 to 7,800 MAD/month** (Échelle 10 base + hazard pay + night shifts / gardes hospitalières).
* **Career Growth Path:** [VERIFIED] Infirmier 1er Grade ➔ Infirmier Principal ➔ Surveillant Général / Cadre de Santé Supérieur.
* **Application Steps:** Registration via `sante.gov.ma` or `emploi-public.ma`, followed by written medical knowledge tests.
* **Required Documents:** [VERIFIED] Legalized ISPITS diploma, CIN copy, certified academic transcripts.

## 3. Category 2: Public Institutions & State Agencies (Établissements Publics)

### A) OFPPT (Office de la Formation Professionnelle et de la Promotion du Travail)
* **Job Title:** [VERIFIED] Formateur en Électromécanique / Informatique / Câblage
* **Sector:** [VERIFIED] Vocational & Professional Training
* **Scope:** [VERIFIED] All national vocational institutes (ISTA / ITA / CMC - Cités des Métiers et des Compétences)
* **Minimum Qualification:** [VERIFIED] Diplôme Technicien Spécialisé (Bac+2) + minimum 3 years verified industrial experience OR Diplôme d'Ingénieur / Master (Bac+5)
* **Estimated Salary Range:** [ESTIMATED] **6,200 to 11,500 MAD/month** (depending on grade and professional seniority).
* **Career Growth Path:** [VERIFIED] Formateur Animateur ➔ Formateur Principal ➔ Directeur Pédagogique d'Établissement.
* **Application Steps:** Submission through `recrutement.ofppt.ma`, technical screening, practical teaching demonstration (*Micro-enseignement*).
* **Required Documents:** [VERIFIED] Detailed CV, copies of degrees, certified CNSS work experience certificates.

### B) Direction Générale de la Sûreté Nationale (DGSN - الشرطة)
* **Job Title:** [VERIFIED] Gardien de la Paix (حارس أمن) / Inspecteur de Police / Officier
* **Sector:** [VERIFIED] National Security & Law Enforcement
* **Minimum Qualification:** [VERIFIED]
  * Gardien de la paix: Baccalauréat (complet)
  * Inspecteur de police: Bac + 1 an d'études supérieures validées
  * Officier de police: Deug / Diplôme Bac+2
* **Age & Physical Requirements:** [VERIFIED] Age 21 to 30; Minimum height 1.73m for men, 1.67m for women; Normal visual acuity without glasses $\ge 15/20$.
* **Estimated Salary Range:** [ESTIMATED] **5,200 to 7,500 MAD/month** (Gardien de la paix) to **8,500+ MAD** (Officiers).
* **Application Steps:** Electronic pre-registration via `concours.dgsn.gov.ma`, physical measurements, written tests, psychological testing, and thorough background check (*Enquête d'habilitation*).
* **Required Documents:** [VERIFIED] Legalized Baccalaureate, CIN, birth certificate, criminal record.

## 4. Category 3: Municipalities & Territorial Collectivities (Collectivités Territoriales)

* **Institutions:** [VERIFIED] Conseils Régionaux, Conseils Préfectoraux, Communes Urbaines et Rurales (ex: Commune de Tanger, Commune de Casablanca).
* **Positions:** [VERIFIED] Technicien 4ème grade (Échelle 8 - Diplôme Technicien OFPPT), Technicien 3ème grade (Échelle 9 - Technicien Spécialisé ISTA), Administrateur 2ème grade (Échelle 11 - Master).
* **Typical Salaries:** [VERIFIED]
  * Échelle 8: **3,800 to 4,200 MAD/month**
  * Échelle 9: **4,400 to 4,900 MAD/month**
  * Échelle 10: **5,500 to 6,200 MAD/month**
  * Échelle 11: **7,800 to 9,200 MAD/month**
* **Application Steps:** Published individually on `emploi-public.ma` under territorial competitions, requiring direct administrative dossier filing at the municipal registry office.
