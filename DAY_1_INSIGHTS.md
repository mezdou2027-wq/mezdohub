## 1. What Users Clearly Want (Empirical Validation)

The behavioral data from Day 1 reveals two unambiguous demand signals:

### A) Immediate, Stress-Relieving Bureaucratic Validation
* **The Evidence:** 
  * `/?tab=dv-lottery` captured **38.7% of all pageviews (24 views)** and triggered **6 canvas image scans**.
  * Article 1 (`shoroot-soura-qor3at-amrika-2027-maroc`) commanded an astounding **3m 12s average reading time**, with **50% of readers clicking through to test their photo immediately**.
* **The User Psychology:**
  Moroccan applicants are terrified of having their Green Card lottery application rejected due to technical photo errors. They do not want general advice; **they want an instant, black-and-white binary verdict: "Is my photo accepted or rejected?"**

### B) Frictionless, Zero-Paywall Document Generation
* **The Evidence:**
  * `/?tab=cv-builder` achieved a **75% completion rate** (3 full PDF resumes generated from 4 started sessions).
  * Selected job sectors were strictly pragmatic: *Câblage Automobile* and *Électromécanique ISTA*.
* **The User Psychology:**
  Technical candidates are tired of complex Canva templates that ask for credit cards upon download or require desktop software. A clean, mobile-friendly ATS CV generator that downloads a pristine PDF in 60 seconds is treated as an indispensable utility.

## 2. What Users Completely Ignore (Current Blind Spots)

The data clearly indicates that certain modules generated near-zero engagement on Day 1:

1. **The Generic Homepage (`/`):**
   * Received only **3 pageviews (4.8% of total traffic)**.
   * *Analytical Finding:* Users do not browse MezdoHub like an encyclopedia. They enter directly through deep-linked utility funnels (`?tab=dv-lottery` or `?tab=cv-builder`). Promoting the homepage URL without a specific tool anchor wastes traffic.
2. **The Freelance & Currency Calculators (`/?tab=freelance-money`):**
   * Received **0 recorded sessions** on Day 1.
   * *Analytical Finding:* While valuable for freelancers, PayPal-to-CIH calculators have no urgent emotional hook compared to the active DV lottery registration window or immediate factory job applications.
3. **The LifeMap Arab Assessment Quiz (`/?tab=lifemap-arab`):**
   * Received **0 recorded sessions**.
   * *Analytical Finding:* Personality and career diagnostic quizzes require high mental energy. Job seekers in urgent need of work or visa validation bypass self-reflection tools.

## 7. Top 3 Hypotheses to Test During the Next 7 Days

To validate our growth model empirically, the product team will monitor the following three falsifiable hypotheses:

### Hypothesis 1 (The DV Viral Loop):
> *"Users who receive a 'PASSED (Green Checkmark)' photo result are 3x more likely to share the tool on WhatsApp than users who fail."*
* **Metric to Track:** Clicks on the WhatsApp share button segmented by photo result (Passed vs. Failed).
* **Decision Rule:** If true, place the primary WhatsApp share CTA directly on the success modal with pre-filled congratulatory text.

### Hypothesis 2 (The Failure-to-Revenue Conversion):
> *"At least 1 out of every 10 users who receive a 'FAILED (Red Flag)' photo result will gladly pay 25 MAD for manual WhatsApp retouching."*
* **Metric to Track:** WhatsApp support chat inquiries generated from the photo result failure screen.
* **Decision Rule:** If conversion reaches > 8%, formalize the manual concierge service into a permanent revenue stream.

### Hypothesis 3 (The OFPPT Resume Trojan Horse):
> *"ISTA candidates who download a factory ATS resume can be converted to apply directly to Tangier plant jobs if a 1-click 'Postuler chez Renault/Lear' button is shown post-download."*
* **Metric to Track:** Click-through rate from the CV builder download completion screen to `/?tab=local-jobs`.
* **Decision Rule:** If CTR exceeds 25%, make job application prompts the default post-download screen.
